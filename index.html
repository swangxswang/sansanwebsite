Kkamangae is a publishing house that started in Seoul in 2015. We create books while enjoying the values of humor and playfulness amidst literature, music, art, and design. Beginning with our first picture book, The Black Dog That Barked Twice, we have published There Is an Egg on the Table, We Can't Survive, Early Summer! Midsummer! Late Summer!, and The Egg Cooking House. We do fun work with energetic people.
